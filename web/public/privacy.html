Solar-kb privacy

Solar-kb is a portfolio project: a chatbot that answers questions about the solar system from NASA's public pages.

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile photo URL.
  • How many questions you asked today, so we can apply the daily limit.
  • For visitors who are not signed in: a one-way scrambled version of your IP address (not the address itself), only to count free questions.
  • A login cookie that keeps you signed in for 7 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Your questions

Questions you type are sent to an AI model provider (Groq or Anthropic) to write the answer. Do not enter personal information in questions.

Delete your data
