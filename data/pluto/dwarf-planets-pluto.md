# Pluto

## Pluto Facts

Pluto is a dwarf planet located in a distant region of our solar system beyond Neptune known as the Kuiper Belt.

Pluto was long considered our ninth planet, but the International Astronomical Union [reclassified Pluto](https://www.iau.org/public/themes/pluto/) as a dwarf planet in 2006. NASA's [New Horizons](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/) was the first spacecraft to explore Pluto up close, flying by in 2015.
