# Saturn

## Facts About Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest planet in our solar system.

Saturn is a massive ball made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It's surrounded by a beautiful ring system. It's the farthest planet from Earth discovered by the unaided human eye.

[Get the Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/)

## Saturn's Shapes

*   ### NASA’s Hubble Tracks New Decagon Encircling Saturn’s South Pole
    
    Recent observations with NASA’s [Hubble Space Telescope](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/) have revealed a giant, evolving, 10-sided atmospheric wave encircling Saturn's south pole. This discovery marks the first time a large, regular-sided jet pattern has been observed in the planet's southern hemisphere. 
    
    [Explore](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/nasas-hubble-tracks-new-decagon-encircling-saturns-south-pole/)
    
*   ### Saturn's Hexagon
    
    In Saturn's northern hemisphere, an enormous spinning hexagon in the clouds at its north pole was first spotted in the 1980s. The hurricane-like vortex has an eye about 50 times larger than a typical Earth hurricane, and a maximum wind speed about double that of the strongest cyclones here.
    
    [Explore](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/science/saturn/hexagon-in-motion/)
