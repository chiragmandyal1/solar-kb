# Cassini Approaches Saturn

**Explanation:** What would it look like to approach the grand planet Saturn? One doesn't have to just imagine -- the [Cassini spacecraft](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/spacecraft/cassini-orbiter/) did just this in 2004, recording thousands of images along the way, and [hundreds of thousands more](https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/cassini-raw-images/) since entering orbit. Some of Cassini's early images have been digitally tweaked, cropped, and compiled into the [featured inspiring video](https://vimeo.com/11386048) which is part of a larger IMAX movie project named [In Saturn's Rings](https://insaturnsrings.com/). In the concluding sequence, [Saturn](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/) looms increasingly large on approach as [cloudy Titan](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/fap/ap090505.html) swoops below. With Saturn [whirling around](https://68.media.tumblr.com/12acf86ba61a04471095cc6ba2c1de78/tumblr_nrowhptkm41t1cy7no1_500.jpg) in the background, Cassini is next depicted flying over [Mimas](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/fap/ap141021.html), with large [Herschel Crater](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/fap/ap100511.html) clearly visible. Saturn's majestic rings then take over the show as Cassini crosses Saturn's [thin ring](https://caps.gsfc.nasa.gov/simpson/kingswood/rings/) plane. [Dark shadows](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap260616.html) of the ring appear on [Saturn itself](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/galleries/pj-saturn/). Finally, the enigmatic ice-geyser moon [Enceladus](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/enceladus/) appears in the distance and then is approached just as the video clip ends. After more than a decade of exploration and discovery, the [Cassini spacecraft](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/about-the-mission/) ran low on fuel in 2017 was directed to [enter Saturn's atmosphere](https://youtu.be/xrGAQCq9BMU), where it surely melted.
