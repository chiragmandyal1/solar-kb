# APOD: 2026 June 16 - Moons, Rings, Shadows, Clouds: Saturn Cassini - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

**Explanation:** While cruising around Saturn, be on the lookout for picturesque arrangements of moons, rings, and shadows. [One such striking sight](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap090505.html) occurred in 2005 and was captured by the then [Saturn-orbiting Cassini](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/) spacecraft. In the featured image, moons [Mimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimas_\(moon\)) (left) and [Tethys](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap170205.html) (right) are visible on either side of [Saturn's thin rings](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/saturns-rings-2/) , which are seen nearly edge-on. Across the top of [Saturn](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/) are dark [shadows](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap120703.html) of the wide rings, exhibiting their impressive complexity. The [violet-light image](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/supreme-beauty/) brings up the texture of the backdrop: [Saturn's clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn#Atmosphere) . Cassini orbited Saturn [from 2004](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap170911.html) until mid-2017, when the [robotic spacecraft](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/the-journey/the-spacecraft/) was directed to [dive](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/93/2a/bc/932abc62ec8cf82d7d93fe639c7cba55.jpg) into Saturn to keep it from [contaminating any moons](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/grand-finale/overview/) .
