# New Sharpest Image of the Sun Uncovers Instability

**Explanation:** What does the new sharpest image of our Sun show? Instability. To be clear, a certain kind of interactive process called the [Kelvin-Helmholtz instability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin%E2%80%93Helmholtz_instability) (KHI). This instability can create [waves and swirls](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-us-canada-63912257) when two streams flow past each other -- in this case variable streams of [solar magnetic plasma](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/heat/big-ideas/big-idea-3-1/). Long hypothesized to occur on the [Sun's surface](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap240615.html), KHI streaks and swirls were confirmed in just-released dramatic high-resolution images taken recently by the [Inouye Solar](https://nso.edu/telescopes/inouye-solar-telescope/) [Telescope](https://youtu.be/vN8cnN7Nlh4) in [Hawaii](https://youtu.be/YPVLrSjKlh8), [USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).  The [featured false-yellow image](https://nso.edu/press-release/nsf-inouye-solar-telescope-enables-major-discovery-of-a-hidden-solar-process/), actually taken in deep blue, is the highest resolution image yet of [the Sun](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap251207.html) in [visible light](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/09_visiblelight/). It spans about the radius of [the Earth](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap260322.html), but its finest details are city sized. Visible are several smooth tops of [changing solar granules](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap200203.html), while the edges of the flower-like structures have been found to harbor multiple [KHI swirls](https://scied.ucar.edu/image/kelvin-helmholtz-clouds). [Future research](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcREdooSerHyBwFsDYNPLDPzuJNwiZ7D_x8TKWp3KvPAQA&s=10) may investigate how the KHI helps move energy and magnetic fields, and may even heat the surrounding [solar corona](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap230516.html).
