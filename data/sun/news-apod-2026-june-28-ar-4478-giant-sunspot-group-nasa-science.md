# APOD: 2026 June 28 - AR 4478: Giant Sunspot Group - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

**Explanation:** Right now, one of the largest sunspot groups in recent history is crossing the Sun. [Active Region 4478](https://www.spaceweatherlive.com/en/solar-activity/region/14478.html) is not only big -- it's violent, showing tangled magnetic fields capable of throwing off huge clouds of particles into the [Solar System](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/) . Some of these [CME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronal_mass_ejection) s might impact the Earth. At the extreme, these [solar storm](https://www.spaceweather.gov/news/large-sunspot-group-rotate-earth-view-24-june) s could cause some [Earth-orbiting satellites to malfunction](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5214/) , the [Earth's atmosphere](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/atmosphere/en/) to slightly distort, and [electrical power grids to surge](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/images/u33/finalBoulderPresentation042611%20%281%29.pdf) . When impacting [Earth's upper atmosphere](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-atmosphere/earths-atmosphere-a-multi-layered-cake/) , these particles can produce [beautiful auroras](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap230122.html) . Pictured here, [AR 4478](https://spaceweathergallery2.com/indiv_upload.php?upload_id=234253) and its dark [sunspot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunspot) s were captured in visible light a few days ago from [Barcelona](https://youtu.be/FGRvP8eoPdw) , [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain) . Almost as large as [AR 3664](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap240513.html) was in 2024, the [AR 4478 sunspot group](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaDSAjilnC5/) is so big that it is [visible just with glasses](https://people.com/thmb/kiAUaJce7MqVr5XwZFuOA3S10MU=/750x0/filters:no_upscale\(\):max_bytes\(150000\):strip_icc\(\):focal\(999x0:1001x2\):format\(webp\)/dog-eclipse-2000-ef0176770ff64e6b913f2af6e8273a24.jpg) specially designed to view [solar eclipse](https://science.nasa.gov/eclipses/future-eclipses/eclipse-2024/) s. This week, [skygazing enthusiasts](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap040808.html) all over the globe will not only be tracking AR 4478 during the day -- but keenly watching night skies for its [corresponding bright auroras](https://www.spaceweather.gov/communities/aurora-dashboard-experimental) .
