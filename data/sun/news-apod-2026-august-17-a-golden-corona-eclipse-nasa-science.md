# APOD: 2026 August 17 - A Golden Corona Eclipse - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

[](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/apod/apod/2026/august/GoldCorona_Santos_960.jpg)

**Explanation:** This total solar eclipse appeared not only poetically beautiful but scientifically interesting. Usually the [solar corona](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2023-may-16-total-eclipse-the-big-corona/) appears white, and to some observers the corona of last week's total solar eclipse did appear [this pearly color](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod/apod-2026-august-14-total-solar-eclipse-from-greenland/). But this time, totality observers in Spain saw a corona that appeared unusually golden. For one reason, from [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain), the totality occurred when the setting Sun was near the horizon. That low, sunlight travels through a large amount of [air which scatters](https://www.optics4kids.org/what-is-optics/scattering/why-is-the-sky-blue-why-are-sunsets-red) out blue light. An unusual amount of [smoke in the air](https://media.istockphoto.com/id/1215895183/photo/portrait-of-a-ginger-cat-the-cat-in-the-medical-face-mask-outdoors-medical-concept.jpg) from nearby [forest fires](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/map/#d:24hrs;@-5.9,35.0,3.1z) acted as a second filter, [further scattering](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod/apod-july-30-red-sun-trough-wildfire-smoke/) the remaining blue tones and deepening the already gold-dominated [light](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/09_visiblelight/). The [HDR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_dynamic_range)\-processed, multiple-exposure [featured image](https://www.instagram.com/p/Db_yz70jS86/) was captured from [Benavente](https://youtu.be/GqtSSkrpk6E), [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain) last week. One thing that did not appear golden was a [hydrogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen-alpha)\-glowing [prominence](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2023-august-1-monster-solar-prominence/) that hovered over the [Sun](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/)'s left edge -- its original [bright pink](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2017-september-12-a-total-solar-eclipse-close-up-in-real-time/) color survived.
