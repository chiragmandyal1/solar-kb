# APOD: 2026 March 31 - Uranus Largest Moon: Titania - NASA Science

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

**Explanation:** Titania's tortured terrain is a mix of canyons, cliffs, and craters. [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/) 's interplanetary robot spacecraft [Voyager 2](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/voyager-2/) passed the largest moon of [Uranus](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/) in 1986 and took the [feature picture](https://www.flickr.com/photos/197038812@N04/53515363728/) . That the [trenches of Titania](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap000930.html) resemble those on another moon of Uranus, [Ariel](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap960303.html) , indicate that Titania underwent some violent surface event possibly related to water [freezing and expanding](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024LPICo3040.1034B/abstract) in its distant past. Although Titania is Uranus's largest moon, it is only about half the radius of [Triton](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap140826.html) - the largest moon of Uranus's sister planet [Neptune](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap210725.html) , which itself is [slightly smaller](https://imgur.com/little-kitten-meets-big-dog-ODwrS) than [Earth's Moon](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap251004.html) . [Titania](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/moons/titania/) , discovered by [William Herschel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Herschel) in 1787, is essentially a large dirty iceball that is composed of about half water-ice and half rock. There is recent speculation that [radioactive heating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decay_heat) melts some underground ice into oceans.
