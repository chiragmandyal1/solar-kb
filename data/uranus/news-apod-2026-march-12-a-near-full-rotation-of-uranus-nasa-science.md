# A Near-Full Rotation of Uranus

**Explanation:** [For the first time](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Webb/Webb_maps_Uranus_s_mysterious_upper_atmosphere)we are witnessing outer planet[Uranus](https://science.nasa.gov/uranus/) take center stage and[pirouette](https://tenor.com/kkzN22NnPWc.gif).Uranus is one of the Solar System’s strangest planets, [lying on its side](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap250914.html) and spinning like a [rotisserie chicken](https://tinyurl.com/rotisserie-chicken).The featured video is composed of over 1000 [spectra](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/science-overview/science-explainers/spectroscopy-101-types-of-spectra-and-spectroscopy/) taken over 15 hours of continuous viewing by [JWST's NIRSpec instrument](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/nirspec/)while Uranus rotates.The data captures the behavior of Uranus’s ionosphere:the ionized layer of a planet’s atmosphere that strongly interacts with [the planet's magnetic field](https://astrobites.org/2020/12/11/a-tour-of-solar-system-magnetospheres/).The aurora’s rosy glow traces the complex interplay between [Uranus's misaligned rotation and magnetic axes](https://www.planetary.org/space-images/the-magnetic-fields-of-uranus-and-neptune).Clouds can be seen as bright spots traveling across the ice giant. The blue-to-red colors represent low-to-high altitudes, showinga brand new three-dimensional view into how energy is distributed throughout the planet’s atmosphere. In the image on the left, everything is framed by [the rings of Uranus](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap971126.html).This is the most detailed look into the atmosphere of Uranus achieved yet!
