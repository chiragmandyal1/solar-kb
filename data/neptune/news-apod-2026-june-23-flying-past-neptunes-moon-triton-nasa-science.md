**Explanation:** What would it look like to fly past [Triton](https://science.nasa.gov/neptune/moons/triton/) , the largest moon of planet [Neptune](https://science.nasa.gov/neptune/) ? Only one spacecraft has ever done this -- and the images of this dramatic encounter have been [gathered](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/icy_moons/neptune/triton/press_release/) into a [video](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/icy_moons/neptune/triton/movie/index.shtml) . In 1989, the [Voyager 2](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/voyager-2/) robotic spacecraft shot through the Neptune system with [cameras](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/icy_moons/neptune/triton/) blazing. [Triton](ap070304.html) is slightly smaller than [Earth's Moon](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/) but has [ice volcanoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triton_\(moon\)#Cryovolcanism) and a surface rich in [frozen nitrogen](https://youtu.be/rM04U5BO3Ug) . The first sequence in [the video](https://www.lpi.usra.edu/icy_moons/neptune/triton/movie/index.shtml) shows Voyager's approach to [Triton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triton_\(moon\)) , which, with the exception of an overall [false green tint](https://www.flickr.com/photos/nasacommons/9467442336/) , appears in approximately true color. The mysterious [cantaloupe terrain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triton_\(moon\)#Cantaloupe_terrain) seen under the spacecraft soon changed from light to dark, with the [terminator](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/terminator) of night crossing [underneath](https://www.shutterstock.com/image-photo/british-shorthair-cat-hiding-under-260nw-1655111092.jpg) . After closest approach, [Voyager](https://stereomoons.blogspot.com/2014/08/triton-at-25.html) pivoted to see the departing moon, now visible as a diminishing [crescent](ap241109.html) . In 2015, the robotic [New Horizons](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/) spacecraft famously [flew past](ap250518.html) [Pluto](https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/pluto/) , an orb of similar size to Triton.
