# APOD: 2026 January 6 - Jupiters Clouds in High Definition from Juno - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

[](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/apod/apod/2026/january/JupiterClouds_JunoThomopoulos_2048.jpg)

**Explanation:** How complex is Jupiter? NASA's Juno mission to Jupiter is finding the Jovian giant to be [more complicated than expected](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6Joupv6f-M) . Jupiter's magnetic field has been [discovered to be](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2018GeoRL..45.2590C/abstract) much different from our [Earth's simple dipole field](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/representation-of-earths-invisible-magnetic-field/) , showing [several poles embedded in a complicated network](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/a-whole-new-jupiter-first-science-results-from-nasas-juno-mission/) more convoluted in the north than the south. Further, Juno's radio measurements show that [Jupiter's atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmosphere_of_Jupiter) shows structure well [below the upper cloud deck](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap240519.html) -- even hundreds of kilometers deep. [Jupiter](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/) 's [newfound complexity](https://www.reddit.com/r/MiniatureSchnauzer/comments/1jf7dir/the_dogs_had_a_great_day/#lightbox) is evident also in southern clouds, as shown in the texture and color enhanced [featured image](https://www.flickr.com/photos/195227719@N04/54996974243/in/pool-apods/) taken last month. There, planet-circling [zones and belts](http://www.planetary.org/multimedia/space-images/jupiter/jupiters-belts-and-zones.html) that dominate near the equator decay into a [complex miasma](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap180521.html) of continent-sized storm swirls. [Juno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_\(spacecraft\)) continues in its looping elliptical orbit, [swooping near the huge planet](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap180226.html) every month and exploring a slightly different sector each time around.
