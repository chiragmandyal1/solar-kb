# APOD: 2025 December 14 - Juno Flyby of Ganymede and Jupiter - NASA Science

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

**Explanation:** What would it be like to fly over the [largest moon](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-moons/ganymede/) in the [Solar System](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/solar-system-facts/) ? In 2021, the robotic [Juno spacecraft](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/juno/) flew past [Jupiter](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/) 's huge moon [Ganymede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganymede_\(moon\)) and took images that have been digitally constructed into a detailed flyby. As the [featured video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CC7OJ7gFLvE) begins, Juno swoops over the two-toned surface of the 5,000-km wide moon, revealing an icy alien landscape filled with grooves and craters. The [grooves](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap960711.html) are likely caused by shifting surface plates, while the craters are caused by [violent impacts](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap010428.html) . Continuing on in its orbit, Juno then performed its 34th [close pass](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap250216.html) over Jupiter's clouds. The [digitally-constructed video](https://www.missionjuno.swri.edu/media-gallery/junos-10th-launch-anniversary?show=fig_610b07d5f5b97167311ead97&m=610b07d5f5b97167311ead97) shows numerous [swirling clouds](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap171128.html) in the north, colorful planet-circling [zones and bands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmosphere_of_Jupiter#/media/File:Jupiter_cloud_bands.svg) across the middle -- featuring several [white-oval cloud](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap170228.html) s from the [String of Pearls](https://astronomynow.com/2017/06/26/jupiters-string-of-pearls/) , and finally more [swirling](https://thumbs.dreamstime.com/b/two-cats-yin-yang-hug-sleep-friendship-love-126599120.jpg) clouds in the south.
