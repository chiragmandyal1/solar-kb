# APOD: 2026 January 18 - Jupiter from the Webb Space Telescope - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

**Explanation:** This infrared view of Jupiter by Webb is illuminating. High-resolution [infrared](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/07_infraredwaves) images of [Jupiter](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/) from the [James Webb Space Telescope](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/james-webb-space-telescope/en/) (Webb) reveal, for example, differences between high-floating bright clouds -- including the [Great Red Spot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Red_Spot) -- and low-lying dark clouds. Also clearly visible in the [featured Webb image](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/31191) are [Jupiter's dust ring](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap220720.html) , bright [auroras at the poles](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap001219.html) , and Jupiter's moons [Amalthea](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-moons/amalthea/) and [Adrastea](https://solarsystem.nasa.gov/moons/jupiter-moons/adrastea/in-depth.amp) . The footprint of large volcanic moon [Io](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap140330.html) 's magnetic funneling of charged particles onto [Jupiter](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap220828.html) is also visible in the southern aurora. Some objects are so bright that light noticeably [diffracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffraction_spike) around [Webb's optics](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/optical-telescope-element-ote/) creating [streaks](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/webbs-diffraction-spikes/) . Webb, which [orbits the Sun](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/orbit/) near the Earth, has a mirror over six meters across making it the [largest astronomical telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_optical_reflecting_telescopes) [ever](https://as2.ftcdn.net/v2/jpg/01/89/49/89/1000_F_189498933_OeutsN0mMlzJGaJZJvkXIsoSCUCFIO7W.jpg) launched -- with over six times [more light-collecting area](https://youtu.be/j3mk6tUokm4) than [Hubble](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/ap090525.html) .
