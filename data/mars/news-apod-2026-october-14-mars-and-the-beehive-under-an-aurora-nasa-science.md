# APOD: 2026 October 14 - Mars and the Beehive Under an Aurora - NASA Science

APOD

Astronomy Picture of the Day

[Discover the cosmos!](https://science.nasa.gov/apod/archive/) Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

[](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/apod/apod/2026/october/2026_10_10_PZ_Jesli%C4%8Dky_01_1500px.png)

**Explanation:** What a cosmic coincidence! In [today’s image](https://www.petrhoralek.com/?p=26662), [Mars](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/) joins the [Beehive cluster](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2022-april-30-m44-the-beehive-cluster/) ([M44](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/science/explore-the-night-sky/hubble-messier-catalog/messier-44/) or Praesepe) below an [auroral curtain](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2026-march-8-the-aurora-tree/) and above a horizon of [happy little trees](https://youtube.com/shorts/1VXZhqbxsng?si=J9_AydZHXiweXx81) near [Lansjärv, Sweden](https://youtu.be/ALGu8Yu_66s?si=HWByw9OqhcsnNX8i). An [eruption off the Sun](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/solar-storms-and-flares/#coronal-mass-ejection) reached Earth on [October 10th, 2026](https://www.spaceweather.gov/news/strong-g3-geomagnetic-storms-expected-10-oct-2026) causing strong [disturbances in Earth’s magnetic field](https://www.spaceweather.gov/phenomena/geomagnetic-storms). [Energetic particles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E3T2ldsdtMg) from the eruption collide with and energize the molecules in Earth’s atmosphere, which release that extra energy as the colorful [aurora](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/auroras/) captured here. Auroras, or guovssahasat in North Sámi, is traditionally viewed with respect by the [Sámi people](https://sweden.se/life/people/sami-in-sweden) indigenous to Sweden and other Arctic regions. Coincidentally, [Mars](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2025-march-23-ancient-ogunquit-beach-on-mars/) [appeared closest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apparent_place) to the center of the Beehive cluster in the sky at the time. Although [bees have been to space](https://texasarchive.org/2024AC06462), the Beehive cluster is actually a swarm of young stars. [Their rotation periods](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.13250) have helped humanity learn about how [stars spin slower over time](http://cfa.harvard.edu/news/stars-spins-reveal-their-ages) as they lose mass through their [stellar winds](https://esahubble.org/wordbank/stellar-wind/).
